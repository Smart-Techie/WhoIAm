Product Venture 🔄 In Development

DOVI

A venture under Malc Nexus Technologies Limited

Students face a very real and frustrating problem: wanting to buy and sell within their campus community — but not knowing who to trust, where to look, or whether their money is safe. DOVI is being designed to solve exactly that. It's a student-focused marketplace built to make buying and selling within student communities simpler, safer, and more trustworthy.

Product Case Study

The Problem

Students struggle to find trustworthy sellers, discover products within their campus, verify vendor reliability, and handle transactions safely.

Users

University students — both buyers looking for deals within campus and student vendors wanting a reliable way to sell.

Key Decisions

Focus on trust mechanisms first. A marketplace only works when buyers feel safe. Trust = the core product.

Lessons Learned

User interviews changed the direction. The biggest pain point wasn't discovery — it was trust and payment safety.

Key Product Areas

Marketplace design Vendor experience Buyer experience Trust mechanisms Order tracking Escrow / payment concepts Save2Own feature Product discovery
